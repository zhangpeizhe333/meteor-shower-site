You may delete your account at any time. Deletion takes effect immediately and is irreversible.

一、如何删除账号（中文）

  1. 1打开「快乐流星雨」App，并登录你的账号。
  2. 2点击底部导航栏的「我的」。
  3. 3点击「我的账号」。
  4. 4点击「删除账号」，并在弹出的二次确认中点击「确认删除」。

删除立即生效，无需等待，也无需联系客服。如果你无法访问 App（例如忘记了密码），也可以发邮件至 zhangpeizhe333@gmail.com，注明你的账号名，我们会在 7 天内为你手动删除。

数据类型处理方式
账号名（用户 ID）与登录凭证立即删除
账号资料（昵称、头像编号等）立即删除
与账号绑定的收藏、偏好设置等数据立即删除
服务器安全日志仅保留最长 90 天后自动删除

除上述安全日志外，我们不保留任何可识别你个人身份的数据。游客模式下使用产生的本地数据不与任何账号关联，卸载 App 即被清除。

2. How to Delete Your Account (English)

  1. 1Open "Meteor Shower Joy" and sign in to your account.
  2. 2Tap the "Profile" tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. 3Tap "My Account".
  4. 4Tap "Delete Account" and confirm in the dialog.

Deletion takes effect immediately, with no waiting period and no need to contact support. If you cannot access the app (e.g. you forgot your password), email zhangpeizhe333@gmail.com with your account name and we will delete it manually within 7 days.

Data typeHandling
Account name (user ID) and credentialsDeleted immediately
Profile data (nickname, avatar index, etc.)Deleted immediately
Favorites and preferences linked to the accountDeleted immediately
Server security logsRetained up to 90 days, then deleted automatically

Except for the security logs above, we retain no data that can identify you. Local data generated in guest mode is not linked to any account and is removed when you uninstall the app.
